- 2026-09-12 — M2 verdict for S1 v1: **not good enough, proceed to shadow anyway**. Full-history backtest (2023-01 → 2026-09, BTCUSDT 15m/1h, 0.1% fee, 0.05% slippage): 60 trades, 36% win rate, expectancy −0.40R; walk-forward out-of-sample (180d train / 60d test, 32-combo grid): 41 trades, −0.35R. Beats the random baseline (−0.58R) but loses. Fees exceed the loss in both runs, so stops are too tight relative to a 0.2% round trip; 22 of 60 exits were the regime rule. Shadow mode (M3) validates the pipeline while S1 iterates: widen stops or use ATR-based stops, soften the regime exit, consider the BNB fee discount (0.075%), then re-run the walk-forward.
- 2026-09-12 — Spot sizing reality: **effective risk per trade = min(risk_pct, notional_cap_pct × stop distance)**. A 1% risk target with a 0.9% stop needs 114% of equity in notional; the 25% cap makes the real risk about 0.22%. The risk manager reports the capped amount and the journal records it. To trade 1% real risk on spot, stops need to be ≥ 4% of price or the cap must rise.
- 2026-09-12 — Enums in TypeScript keep **string values wherever they cross a boundary** (journal columns, Redis events, Python fixtures); numeric defaults only for enums that never leave the process.
- 2026-09-12 — Live regime invalidation closes at the regime candle's close price; the backtester closes at the next entry-bar open. The difference is one 15m gap and is accepted for shadow; paper mode (M5) will fill at next open to match the backtester exactly.
