- 2026-09-12 — Exchange: **Binance**. Best liquidity, free spot + futures testnets, ccxt support in both languages.
- 2026-09-12 — Market: **spot first, futures later**. No liquidation risk while the bot is being proven; adapter interface keeps futures a drop-in.

- 2026-09-12 — Timeframes: **default 15m entries with a 1h regime filter (S3: 5m entries, 15m range), all configurable per strategy instance** in a config file, no code change to switch. Matches how you traded; keeps trade count high enough to learn from the journal; lets the same strategy be compared across timeframes.

S1 support bounce on BTCUSDT; the bot marks buy points, projected sell points and follows them to an outcome in the database, with a dashboard and Telegram notifications. Paper wallet, testnet and live come only after two weeks of reviewed shadow signals. Reason: prove the bot sees what you would have seen before any money or simulated money is involved.
- 2026-09-12 — Run modes: **shadow → paper → testnet → live**, one `MODE` setting; shadow and paper share a code path, paper adds the simulated wallet.
- 2026-09-12 — Journal: **every signal is recorded, including rejected ones**, with projection at signal time and outcome written back on close. This is the dataset for judging strategies.
- 2026-09-12 — Protection: **exchange-side OCO always**; two half-size OCOs for TP1/TP2; bot only ever ratchets them. No open position without an exchange stop.

- 2026-09-12 — M2 verdict for S1 v1: **not good enough, proceed to shadow anyway**. Full-history backtest (2023-01 → 2026-09, BTCUSDT 15m/1h, 0.1% fee, 0.05% slippage): 60 trades, 36% win rate, expectancy −0.40R; walk-forward out-of-sample (180d train / 60d test, 32-combo grid): 41 trades, −0.35R. - 2026-09-12 — Spot sizing reality: **effective risk per trade = min(risk_pct, notional_cap_pct × stop distance)**. A 1% risk target with a 0.9% stop needs 114% of equity in notional; the 25% cap makes the real risk about 0.22%. The risk manager reports the capped amount and the journal records it. To trade 1% real risk on spot, stops need to be ≥ 4% of price or the cap must rise.
- 2026-09-12 — Enums in TypeScript keep **string values wherever they cross a boundary** (journal columns, Redis events, Python fixtures); numeric defaults only for enums that never leave the process.
- 2026-09-12 — Live regime invalidation closes at the regime candle's close price; the backtester closes at the next entry-bar open. The difference is one 15m gap and is accepted for shadow; paper mode (M5) will fill at next open to match the backtester exactly.
- 2026-09-12 — Shadow soak diagnosis: hours without a signal is expected. Every evaluation so far was blocked by the 1h regime filter (all ten symbols below EMA50 < EMA200); a 30-day replay of all ten S1 instances yields 5 signals total (~1 per 6 days across the basket). Loosening `wick_min_pct` / `rsi_max` or the `close > EMA50` half of the trend gate is strategy iteration, not a bug, and changes what the shadow-vs-backtest comparison measures.
- 2026-09-12 — Paper mode (M5): the entry is **pending until the next entry-timeframe candle**, fills at that open plus slippage, is sized at that open (step-rounded, minimums checked), and `risk_amount = (fill − stop) × qty`, so `pnl == r_multiple × risk_amount` holds exactly and `research compare --mode paper` can demand R equality with the backtester. A fill beyond the stop or target cancels the entry (`gap_at_fill`), as in the backtester. Exit fills go through the adapter too, so the wallet sees every sell.
- 2026-09-12 — Equity snapshot semantics: `balance_quote` = cash + cost of open positions, `unrealised` = Σ remaining_qty × (mark − entry); equity = sum of both; drawdown from the running peak. Sizing uses cash + open cost.
- 2026-09-12 — Several engines, one journal: consumer groups are per mode (`engine:<mode>`) so a paper engine can run beside the shadow soak and see every signal and command; the **first mode to act on a signal owns its outcome columns** (`WHERE mode IS NULL OR mode = current`), every mode's own outcomes live in `positions`, and risk history (daily R, consecutive losses) is read from `positions`, not `signals`.
- 2026-09-12 — Exit params `trail_atr_k: null` / `tp_ratchet_atr: null` mean "off" in both exit-policy implementations and the signal contract (needed by S3, mean reversion).
- 2026-09-12 — Backtester look-ahead fix: regime invalidation was evaluated at bar i's open when the regime bar closed together with bar i; it now acts one entry bar later (`regime_idx[i-1] != regime_idx[i-2]`), the first bar at which that close was known. S1 backtest numbers shift slightly; the would-have-won replay uses the same rule.
- 2026-09-12 — S3 "range breaking" exit is the price proxy `invalidation_level = range_low` (regime-tf close below the range low), not the plan's 1h ADX > 25: the engine's invalidation is price-based and needs no indicator code. S3 keeps `max_fee_r` at 0.5 (S1/S2: 0.25) because its stop sits just under the range low.
- 2026-09-12 — Binance protection (M6): two sell OCOs (A: tp1 / stop on the TP1 fraction, B: tp / stop on the rest), stop-limit 0.2% under the stop, replaced only when the stop rose ≥ 1 tick and 0.05%, the target changed or the quantity changed, at most once per candle. OCO rejected because the market is already through the stop or target → that quantity is sold at market and alerted; rejected for another reason → a lone stop-loss-limit (upgraded to an OCO next candle); three failed placements → the whole position is sold at market (`order_rejected`) and force-closed. Exits decided by the exit policy first consume fills the exchange already made, then cancel and sell the remainder at market.
- 2026-09-12 — Live gate (M7): `engine_state.entries_enabled` defaults to **false** for `mode = live` and true elsewhere; a live engine boots as a dry run (balances, filters, user stream, reconciliation) and is armed with the `entries_on` command from the Controls page. - 2026-09-12 — Reconciliation policy: an open order on the account that we did not place pauses new entries (it is never cancelled — it may be yours); a position without an exchange stop is re-protected on the spot; a base balance below what positions hold raises `reconcile_mismatch` without pausing.

- 2026-09-13 — Pattern research (`research label` → `explain` → `forward`, [DATA.md](DATA.md#pattern-research-what-preceded-good-trades)): the "+90% accuracy" goal was replaced by **positive out-of-sample expectancy after fees on ≥ 300 trades**, because a trade at every bar is a coin flip gross of fees (1h: +0.02 R) and fees alone cost 0.14 R (1h, 1.5 ATR stop) to 0.76 R (15m, 1 ATR stop) per trade. No single condition is robust in any setting. Four rule pairs survive the 2024–2026 test years on 1h bars, every year and most coins positive, at +0.08 to +0.14 R per trade: buy a coin down > 35% over 30 days once it is 7% off its 20-bar low, buy high-ATR coins on a day BTC fell > 2.9%, short a coin at a fresh 30-day low after a 27–35% fall. Nothing on 15m is robust. Findings in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md#pattern-research-findings). These are candidates, not strategies: they go through a strategy class + `research backtest`/`walkforward` on the 31-coin universe before any instance is configured.

- 2026-09-13 — Win rate is a dial, not a target: relabelling the same S4 entries with a 1-ATR target and 3-ATR stop gives an 80% win rate and +0.05 R per trade; with the 2:1 target it wins 47% and makes +0.26 R. A random entry already wins 69% with the wide-stop geometry and loses money. The strategy goal is therefore expectancy after fees with several signals a day across the basket, not a win-rate threshold. To find the best exits for that, S4's Optuna search space now covers the trailing stop (`trail_atr_k` none/1.5/2.5/4), where trailing starts (`tp1_r`), breakeven on/off, target ratchet on/off and the time limit, and `scripts/s4-optimize.sh` runs it per coin in parallel (40 trials per 180-day training window, 90-day test windows, since 2022). - 2026-09-13 — Optuna search verdict: **more search, worse result.** With 40 trials per training window over entry thresholds and five exit knobs, S4's pooled out-of-sample expectancy fell to +0.042 R on 5,719 trades, against +0.114 R for the six-combination grid and +0.203 R for the untouched default. The optimiser could not settle (no trailing in 41% of windows, 1.5 ATR in 26%, 4 ATR in 25%; drop thresholds from 2% to 10%), and every switch was paid for in the next quarter. Parameters stay fixed; `research optimize` is a diagnostic, not the source of settings. Exit variants with fixed entries (28 coins, 2024→): pure barriers +0.203 R/trade (1,107 trades), trail 4 ATR from +1R +0.186 R, runner (trail 2.5 ATR from +1R, target ratchets) +0.186 R on 1,249 trades and the highest total (+232 R, profit factor 1.42, 53% wins), partial take-profit at +1R +0.125 R (worst: it sells the winners early). **Shadow instances switched to the runner exits**, because the user wants trailing stops and more trades and it costs nothing in total return; drawdown is a little deeper (−24 R vs −18 R).
